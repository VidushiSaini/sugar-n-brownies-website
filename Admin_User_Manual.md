# Bakery Admin Dashboard User Manual

Welcome to your Bakery Admin Dashboard! This tool allows you to control the content on your website without needing to code. 

**Accessing the Dashboard:** Open `admin.html` in your web browser while your backend server is running.

---

## 1. Menu Editor Tab
This is where you manage all the products shown on your `menu.html` page.

- **Categories:** Click the category tabs at the top (e.g., Cakes, Brownies) to view items in that category.
- **Editing an Item:** You can change the Title, Price, Quantity, Weight, and Description directly in the text boxes.
- **Dietary Tags:** Check boxes for Vegan, Gluten-Free, Eggless, etc. Use the **"+ Add Custom Tag"** box to type a specific tag (e.g., "Keto"). These appear as beautiful pills on the public menu.
- **Images:** Click the "Choose File" button below an item's picture to upload a new photo.
- **Saving:** Click the **"Update Item"** button on the specific card you just edited.
- **Deleting:** Click the **Red Trash Icon** on an item to permanently delete it. To delete an entire category, click the red **Delete Category** pill at the top.
- **Adding Items/Categories:** Use the **"+ Add Item"** and **"+ New Category"** buttons at the top of the grid to expand your menu.

> [!NOTE]
> **Where it reflects:** All changes made here instantly update the public **Menu Page (`menu.html`)** grid and product popups.

---

## 2. Page Content Tab
This section controls the text and large images on your website.

- **Website Banners:** Upload images for your "Homepage Hero Banner" and "Menu Page Banner". 
- **About Us Section:** Edit the title, subtitle, and paragraph text that tells your bakery's story.

> [!NOTE]
> **Where it reflects:** 
> - The Homepage Banner changes the large background image at the top of `index.html`.
> - The Menu Banner changes the wide image at the top of `menu.html`.
> - The About Us text updates the story section on `index.html` and `about.html`.

---

## 3. Best Sellers Tab
Manage the highlights on your homepage.

- This tab shows a grid of cards similar to the Menu Editor.
- Update the titles, prices, and images of your most popular items.
- Click **"Update Item"** to save.

> [!NOTE]
> **Where it reflects:** These items are displayed exclusively in the "Our Bestsellers" section on your **Home Page (`index.html`)**.

---

## 4. Business Operations Tab
Control your behind-the-scenes data.

- **Store Hours:** Update your opening and closing times for each day of the week.
- **Contact Info:** Update your phone number, email, and social media links.
- Click **"Update Operations"** to save.

> [!NOTE]
> **Where it reflects:** Currently stored in the database for future updates to your website's footer and contact pages.
